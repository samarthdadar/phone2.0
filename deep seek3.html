Our Story

Our Journey Since 2006

Established in 2006

Bhaskar Educational Store was founded in 2006 with a simple mission: to provide students across India with high-quality, affordable mathematical instruments that enhance their learning experience. What started as a small workshop in Delhi has grown into a leading manufacturer serving over 500 schools nationwide.

Our commitment to quality earned us the ISO 9001:2015 certification, and our dedication to innovation has led to the development of proprietary technologies like our self-centering compass mechanism. Today, we're proud to be India's trusted name in mathematical instruments.

Product Care Guide

Tips to maintain your mathematical instruments

Scale Maintenance

  • Clean with dry cloth after use
  • Store in protective case when not in use
  • Avoid exposing to extreme temperatures

Compass Care

  • Keep pivot point lubricated
  • Store with protective cap on needle
  • Adjust tension screw as needed
